Who We Are

Med Repair is a professional medical equipment maintenance company dedicated to supporting hospitals, clinics, laboratories, and diagnostic centers. We specialize in ensuring that critical healthcare equipment operates safely, reliably, and efficiently. Our team is committed to delivering structured, contract-based maintenance solutions that help healthcare providers focus on patient care while we handle the technical performance of their equipment.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive preventive and corrective maintenance services under a structured monthly contract model. Our services include scheduled inspections, performance testing, calibration support, fault diagnosis, emergency repairs, and detailed technical reporting.

Our objective is to:

  • Reduce Equipment downtime
  • Extend equipment lifespan
  • Improve operational reliability
  • Provide predictable and controlled maintenance costs

Our Services includes:

  • Preventive maintenance for medical devices
  • Corrective repairs and fault diagnosis
  • X-ray and ultrasound system servicing
  • Laboratory analyzer maintenance
  • Patient monitor and ECG support
  • Dental and sterilization equipment servicing
  • Dialysis and maternity equipment support
  • Maternity Equipment
